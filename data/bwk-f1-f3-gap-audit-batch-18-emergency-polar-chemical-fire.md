## 2. Detailed source findings

### GAP-01 — Safety Committee

- F3 T4 Q85 is a related answer about the shipboard safety officer. It includes chairing or attending monthly meetings, minutes and follow-up actions.
- F3 T3 Q21 includes monthly safety-meeting discussion of anonymous near misses.
- Neither inspected answer supplies the full committee constitution, role / functions, member representation, agenda, minutes and action-tracking sequence requested by the BWK old-paper question.
- **Conclusion:** do not mark the gap closed. F3 provides source-supported building blocks only.

### GAP-02 — Polar navigation systems and existing-ship exemptions

- F1 T6 Polar Code navigation content is a useful partial source for Chapter 9 equipment requirements.
- F3 T8 Q52 gives general Polar Code structure, including safety of navigation, but not the detailed requested list or exemptions.
- The original question specifically asks both navigation-system requirements and exemptions allowed for existing ships. The second limb was not located in the inspected F1/F3 passages.
- **Conclusion:** partially supported; the exemptions limb remains open. Do not infer exemption conditions from construction-date fragments without checking the exact Polar Code transitional provisions and applicability.

### GAP-03 — Chemical-tanker cargo-space fire contingency plan

- BWK E14 is an oil/NLS spillage plan; it is not the missing cargo-space fire procedure.
- F3 T8 Q39 lists fixed fire-fighting system categories; T8 Q83 is an explosion on tanker deck scenario. These provide equipment / scenario context only.
- No complete response sequence for a fire in cargo spaces of IMO Type 1 and Type 2 chemical tankers was established in inspected F1/F3 passages.
- **Conclusion:** gap remains open. Do not assemble a full operational procedure by stitching together generic fire-system notes.
