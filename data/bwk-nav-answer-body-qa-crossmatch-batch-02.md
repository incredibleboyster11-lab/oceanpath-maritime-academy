## 3. Important scope and accuracy flags

- BWK V1–V4 were inspected in the source pages around 27–31. They cover VTS purpose/services, ship reporting systems, Indian INSPIRES/INDSAR descriptions and ship's routeing.
- BWK R2/R3/R9/R15 are SAR organisation/response cards; V3 is ship reporting and Indian reporting systems. The link to NAV AIS/LRIT is conceptual and complementary, not a duplicate.
- Statements about VTS communications, SOLAS references, IMO routeing, Indian reporting thresholds, and SAR organisation are source-derived only at this stage. They have not been checked against current primary regulations or official Indian authority notices.
- The source's own exam-frequency labels and paper counts are not independently audited here and must not be marketed as verified prediction statistics.
- Personal/surveyor material remains excluded from public migration pending privacy review.
