## 3. Important technical / currency flags

1. **Pilot-ladder regulatory currency:** F3 T8 Q17 cites SOLAS V/23, Res. A.1045(27), ISO 799-1 and new amendments MSC.572(110) / MSC.576(110), with stated application dates in 2028 and 2029. This is a source claim requiring verification against the official adopted texts, entry-into-force provisions and applicability before public release.
2. **Different detail levels:** F1 T7 Q30 gives dimensions and rigging details; F3 T8 Q17 adds certificate, renewal and inspection requirements. Do not merge the two into one answer without checking the governing current standard.
3. **Pilot boarding safety:** BWK P4 gives the ship-handling manoeuvre and environmental factors; F1 T7 Q10 and F3 T8 Q17/Q-related ladder material give transfer-equipment safety. A public unit should link these as separate phases: prepare the ship, rig and inspect the arrangement, manoeuvre for boarding, maintain communications, complete the exchange.
4. **Command and responsibility:** BWK P2/P3 emphasise that the Master remains in command and the pilot's presence does not relieve the Master/OOW of duties. Preserve this as a core principle and verify exact wording against current STCW / national requirements before publication.
