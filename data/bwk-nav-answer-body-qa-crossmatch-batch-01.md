### Source-based observations

- BWK H2 describes turning-circle terms and manoeuvring characteristics, including advance, transfer, tactical diameter, drift angle and IMO turning-circle criteria (printed pp.72–73).
- NAV’s controlled-radius turn bank gives a procedural/numerical method: calculate course change and radius, determine WOP/WOL, monitor the turn with radar/VRM and ROTI, and account for current (printed pp.192–206).
- Similar words such as “turn”, “rudder” and “rate of turn” are not sufficient evidence of duplication. The underlying exam task and method must match.
