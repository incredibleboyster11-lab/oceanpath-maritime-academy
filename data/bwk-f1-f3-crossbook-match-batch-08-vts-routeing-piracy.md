### Topic 2 observations

1. The source material correctly separates three related but distinct SOLAS Chapter V subjects: ship routeing (Regulation 10), ship reporting systems (Regulation 11) and VTS (Regulation 12). This distinction should remain explicit in any common learning architecture.
2. BWK V2 has a fuller VTS service taxonomy: Information Service, Navigational Assistance Service and Traffic Organisation Service. F1 T9 Q13 is a concise regulatory match, not a substitute for the operational detail.
3. BWK V3 and F1 T9 Q52/Q55/Q56 overlap in report types and Indian systems. F1 includes operational/reporting-centre context; BWK includes the principles and INSPIRES/INDSAR comparison.
4. BWK V4 and F1 T5 Q2 are a strong match on routeing purpose and measures. F1's additional Rule 10 scenarios should be linked as application questions rather than merged into the basic routeing taxonomy.
5. F1's reporting-system questions and piracy reporting questions are related but should not be merged: routine ship reporting, VTS reporting and security/piracy incident reporting have different purposes and authorities.

### Topic 12 observations and review flags

1. **Material currency difference:** BWK Z1/Z2 are explicitly titled BMP5. F3 T3 Q61 states that BMP Maritime Security (2025) replaces BMP5. This is a source-to-source difference, not permission to overwrite the BWK answer.
2. BWK Z1 contains detailed SPM, citadel, watchkeeping, barriers and attack-response steps. F3 T3 Q61 gives a shorter current-framework summary; F1 T9 Q53/Q54 adds reporting-centre and route-reporting details.
3. F3 T3 Q59 (SSAS) and Q60 (SSA) are supporting security-system concepts, not duplicates of the whole piracy-prevention answer.
4. F1 T9 Q11 is a useful reporting-centre cross-link; it is not a full BMP5/BMP 2025 ship-protection answer.
5. The BWK exam tip says the Indian Ocean High Risk Area was discontinued from 1 January 2023 and advises checking current area limits. Do not reuse any area boundary as current without checking the latest official industry guidance.
6. All public use requires checking current BMP edition, regional threat-area definitions, UKMTO/MSCHOA procedures, flag/company requirements and any relevant official updates. No external regulatory validation is claimed in this batch.
