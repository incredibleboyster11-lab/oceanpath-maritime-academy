## 3. Source-derived observations and review flags

1. **STS area vs approach method:** BWK S1 is a location-selection and preparation question; S2 and S3 are distinct manoeuvring methods. Do not merge them into one generic STS procedure.
2. **Parallel vs angled approach:** BWK S2 describes the slower parallel approach; S3 describes the angled approach. Keep separate diagrams, speed management and interaction hazards.
3. **Dry-dock flooding:** BWK S4 and F3 T6 Q7 strongly overlap. F3 adds a staged stop after flooding begins to check for leaks before full flooding. Keep that as a clearly attributed source detail pending technical review.
4. **Critical period:** F1 T7 Q62 and F3 T6 Q46 discuss stability / virtual loss of GM while the ship takes the blocks. This is related to, but not identical with, the BWK pre-flooding checklist.
5. **Dry-dock emergencies:** BWK S5 lists fire, personal injury, enclosed-space accidents and flooding. F3 Q94 addresses a fatal worker accident, a distinct incident-response scenario with authority notification and evidence preservation.
6. **Side-shell renewal:** BWK S6 gives source-specific minimum dimensions, seam cut-back and plate-thickness tolerance. F3's shell-expansion / UTM / scantling answers support the process but do not automatically validate those dimensions for every vessel or class.
7. **STS regulatory references:** BWK cites MARPOL Annex I Reg. 41 for an approved STS operations plan for oil tankers. Verify current wording, applicability and amendments against official instruments before public release.
8. **Dry-dock technical content:** Stability, block loads, upthrust, virtual loss of GM, flooding checks, steel renewal and UTM are safety-critical. Qualified Chief Engineer / Naval Architect / Class review is required before public learning use.
