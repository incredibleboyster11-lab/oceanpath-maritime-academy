# BWK–F1–F3 Cross-book Concept Register — Draft 01

**Created:** 15 November 2026  
**Scope:** Internal deduplication register compiled from cross-match batches 01–04.  
**Source corpus:** User-authored BWK (144 pp.), F1 (534 pp.) and F3 (387 pp.) PDFs.  
**Purpose:** Preserve provenance and identify where future learning units can share a common concept while retaining source-specific exam wording and operational detail.

## Status key

- **Strong overlap:** same core question/objective; content can be linked or considered for a common core with source-specific variants.
- **Complementary:** related objective but different scope; retain distinct learning units and link them.
- **Related only:** useful cross-reference, not a duplicate.
- **Unmatched in inspected scope:** no equivalent found in the inspected material; not a claim that no such content exists anywhere in the corpus.
- **Review required:** technical, regulatory, class, privacy or rights review is still needed before public release.

## 3. Cross-source differences requiring explicit review

1. **Beaching:** BWK E9 recommends high-water beaching on a falling tide; F1/F3 beaching answers prefer a rising tide / close to high water. Preserve the difference and obtain qualified review.
2. **Emergency towing:** BWK/F1/F3 answers use different ship-type and construction-date scopes. Verify SOLAS II-1/3-4 and current IMO circulars before harmonising.
3. **Grounding / stranding:** source answers do not use the terms identically; preserve the source definitions pending review.
4. **Polar Code:** IMO MSC.538(107) amendments effective 1 January 2026 require a currency review of navigation/voyage-planning answers. Check ship category, vessel type, tonnage, voyage and construction date.
5. **Steel renewal:** side-shell insert dimensions, thickness diminution and class criteria are not universal values. Any integrated learning unit must identify the governing class/approved repair specification and require qualified review.

## 5. Limitations

- The register compiles candidate cross-matches from four batches; it is not a full deduplication of all nine books.
- Absence of a match means only that no equivalent was established in the inspected scope.
- Source-declared exam frequency labels have not been independently verified against every original paper.
- Presence and similarity checks are not technical accuracy checks.
- No public learning unit is approved by this register. The original PDFs remain read-only; this file is an internal derivative audit artifact.
