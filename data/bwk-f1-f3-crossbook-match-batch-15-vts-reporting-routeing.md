## 3. Source-derived observations and review flags

1. **SOLAS chapter V separation:** BWK groups the themes but explicitly distinguishes ship routeing (V/10), ship reporting systems (V/11) and VTS (V/12). F1 reinforces the same regulatory separation. Keep these as three related but distinct concepts.
2. **VTS vs VTMS:** BWK describes VTS as the service and VTMS as a system integration term in its discussion. F1 T9 Q26 explicitly states “VTS is the service; VTMS is the system used to provide it” and notes Indian usage. Retain source attribution and verify local terminology against the relevant port / authority.
3. **VTS authority:** BWK states that navigational decisions remain with the Master. F1's VTS material also says ships comply with the service while navigation remains a shipboard responsibility. Do not imply VTS assumes command of the ship.
4. **Ship reporting:** F1 supplies extensive oral coverage of INDSAR, INSPIRES, AMVER, AUSREP, JASREP and STRAITREP, including report types. Current ship-size thresholds and mandatory status can change; verify with current DGS Merchant Shipping Notices and ALRS Vol. 6 before publication.
5. **Indian reporting systems:** BWK's source table and F1 answers include system authorities and tonnage thresholds. Where the source passages differ or use simplified descriptions, preserve the difference in the audit and do not silently reconcile.
6. **Routeing vs reporting:** A ship reporting system is not itself a routeing measure. F1 explicitly separates SOLAS V/10 routeing, V/11 reporting and V/12 VTS. Preserve that distinction in any learning architecture.
7. **F3 search limitation:** No F3 direct match was surfaced by the targeted searches. This remains an inspected-search limitation, not a definitive absence claim.
