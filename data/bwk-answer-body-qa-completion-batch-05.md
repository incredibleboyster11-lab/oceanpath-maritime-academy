## Cross-match with NAV — findings and limits

No exact duplicate has been established between the inspected BWK answer units and the previously inspected NAV units. This is a conservative finding, not proof that no overlap exists.

Examples of related but distinct coverage:
- BWK E1–E3 emergency planning and muster arrangements are not duplicates of NAV's equipment-specific navigation aids.
- BWK M1–M3 mooring methods are not duplicates of NAV's controlled-radius / ROTI calculation units.
- BWK W1–W13 watchkeeping and COLREG units may reference radar, ECDIS and navigation equipment, but their learning objectives are bridge watch organisation, COLREG interpretation and operational conduct.
- BWK S1–S6 STS and dry-dock operations include navigation/shiphandling considerations; NAV equipment knowledge is supporting context rather than an identical answer.
- BWK I1–I5 Polar Code / ice operations involve limitations of navigation systems, but are not the same as NAV's general GPS/GNSS/ECDIS/AIS/VDR equipment questions.

## Regulatory / quality boundary

This pass confirms that answer bodies are present at the mapped locations. It does **not** certify technical correctness, current regulatory status, completeness against every past-paper wording, or operational suitability.

A source example: the BWK E1 answer cites IMO Assembly Resolution A.1072(28), adopted 4 December 2013, which revised the integrated contingency-planning guidelines and revoked A.852(20) from 1 July 2014. The official IMO-hosted resolution was located during this project pass. The IMO itself cautions that current resolution status may not always be ascertainable from its index and advises consultation with the national maritime administration. This is a verification lead, not a blanket approval of all E1 wording.

Public migration remains blocked on component-level rights review, qualified technical review, privacy screening and content governance. Surveyor/oral-report personal details are excluded from public derivatives pending privacy review.
