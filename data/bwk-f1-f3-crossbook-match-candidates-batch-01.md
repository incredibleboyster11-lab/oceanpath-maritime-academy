## Match classifications

- **Same objective / strong overlap:** questions target substantially the same operational knowledge; answers may still differ in depth, scenario or emphasis.
- **Related / complementary:** one source covers a narrower scenario, procedural detail or adjacent knowledge.
- **No match established:** inspected material does not support a match.
- This is a candidate cross-match, not a statement that one answer can replace another.

## Important source differences — preserve, do not silently reconcile

1. BWK E9 p.46 describes beaching at high water on a falling tide; F1 T7 Q25 p.267 prefers a rising tide / close to high water; F3 T7 Q25 p.174 says preferably rising tide. These differences require technical review against the intended scenario and authoritative guidance. Do not merge into one definitive instruction yet.
2. BWK T1/T2 and F1/F3 ETA answers include different scope statements and construction-date thresholds. The regulatory baseline must be checked against the latest SOLAS amendments before publishing.
3. BWK E8 and F3 T7 Q26 use different explanatory distinctions for stranding. Preserve source wording in the comparison record; technical editor to determine the final teaching formulation.
4. F1 T7 Q2 lists several moor types, while BWK M1/M2 and F3 T7 Q7 focus on running/standing moors. This is not a full duplicate of the broader mooring taxonomy.

## Official regulatory watch — emergency towing and mooring

The IMO reports that MSC 110 (June 2025) approved MSC.1/Circ.1691 (interim emergency towing arrangements for non-tankers), MSC.1/Circ.1175/Rev.2 (revised shipboard towing/mooring equipment guidance), and MSC.1/Circ.1255/Rev.1 (revised emergency towing procedure guidelines). This is a clear update lead for the ETA/ETP content; obtain and inspect the actual circular texts and check applicability/effective dates before changing answers. Source: https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/meetingsummaries/pages/msc-110th-session.aspx

IMO also states amended SOLAS II-1/3-8 under MSC.474(102) entered into force on 1 January 2024 and references MSC.1/Circ.1619 and MSC.1/Circ.1620 for mooring design and inspection/maintenance. Source: https://www.imo.org/en/ourwork/safety/pages/safemooring.aspx

This is not a complete regulatory verification. The IMO notes that current resolution status may need confirmation with the national maritime administration.
