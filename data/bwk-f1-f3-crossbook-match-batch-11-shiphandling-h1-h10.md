## 3. Source-derived observations and review flags

1. BWK H1 gives the position of the pivot point in stationary, headway, sternway and at-anchor conditions, and explains its wandering. F1 T7 Q21 uses the pivot point to explain turning lever; the concepts are connected but not duplicates.
2. BWK H2 contains turning-circle definitions and source-stated IMO limits. F3 T7 Q39 focuses on factors affecting the turning circle and rudder cycling. Keep the formal terms and practical factors together through cross-links, not by deleting either treatment.
3. BWK H3 includes Barrass approximations for open water and confined channels. F1 T7 Q35 and F3 T7 Q12 give effects / actions. Verify formula applicability and assumptions before publication; do not present a simplified approximation as universally accurate.
4. BWK H4 and H5 have strong overlap with F3 T7 Q12, but the BWK diagrams and staged interaction explanation are richer.
5. BWK H6 passing moored ships is a distinct operational hazard. No direct equivalent was established in inspected F1/F3 pages.
6. BWK H8 and H9 are separate wind and current effects. No complete matching answer was established in the inspected F1/F3 passages.
7. BWK H10 connects to F1 short-round-turn questions and F3 transverse-thrust explanation. Preserve CPP and fixed-pitch differences rather than treating all propeller systems as interchangeable.
