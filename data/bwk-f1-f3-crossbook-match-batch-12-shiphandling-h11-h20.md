## 3. Review flags

1. **CPP vs fixed-pitch:** BWK H11 and H10 distinguish a CPP/VPP's constant shaft rotation from fixed-pitch reversal behaviour. F1 short-turn answers are specifically RH fixed-pitch examples. Never merge these into a generic propeller procedure.
2. **Twin-screw:** no direct F1/F3 twin-screw answer was established in inspected passages. BWK remains the source for differential shaft operation and its manoeuvring effects.
3. **Short round turn:** F1 provides RH fixed-pitch examples; BWK H14 explicitly covers both RH and LH propellers and channel-side selection. Preserve the missing LH treatment in any derivative.
4. **Rolling formulae:** BWK gives a natural roll-period relationship and synchronism distinctions; F3 T5 Q29 supplies additional formula context. Formula assumptions and units require technical review.
5. **Pooping / broaching / surf-riding:** F3 T5 Q28 adds surf-riding and a speed-related criterion, while BWK H16 explains pooping and broaching. Verify MSC.1/Circ.1228 text and applicability before public release.
6. **Following-sea capsizing:** BWK H17 includes stability loss on the wave crest, reduced steering, cargo shift, synchronous and parametric rolling. F3's two inspected answers support parts, not the whole response.
7. **Heavy weather:** F3 T5 Q25/Q26 cover preparation and ballast, not the full ship-handling choices and turning manoeuvre in BWK H18.
8. **Stopping vs rudder cycling:** F1 Q20 discusses stopping distance and wheelhouse poster content. It does not replace BWK's stepwise rudder-cycling method.
9. **Kick ahead:** F1 short-turn manoeuvres contain ahead kicks, but BWK H20's dedicated bare-steerage-way technique remains a distinct concept.
