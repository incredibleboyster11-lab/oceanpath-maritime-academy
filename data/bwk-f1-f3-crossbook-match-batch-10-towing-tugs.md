## 3. Important source differences / review flags

1. **ETA terminology and applicability:** BWK/F1/F3 all distinguish Emergency Towing Arrangement from Estimated Time of Arrival. Their summaries are not a substitute for checking the current SOLAS text and applicable IMO guidance.
2. **Non-tanker procedure:** BWK T2/T3 explain the ship-specific ETP/ETB contents and operational decision matrix. F1/F3 inspected passages only summarise ETP applicability; they do not provide the same full booklet structure.
3. **Strength values:** F1 and F3 give SWL values by tanker deadweight. These figures and their exact scope must be checked against the applicable IMO resolution, vessel construction date and Administration-approved arrangement before publication.
4. **Towing-pattern decision matrix:** BWK includes 1-F/1-A, 2-F/2-A and 3-F/3-A patterns and a condition matrix. No equivalent full matrix was established in the inspected F1/F3 passages.
5. **Girting terminology:** BWK uses “gob rope”; F3 contains “gog rope” in one passage and “gob rope” in another. Preserve the source wording and verify the correct term against a qualified towing reference.
6. **Tug-type taxonomy:** BWK's core comparison is conventional / tractor / ASD. F3 adds reverse tractor, rotor, combi, escort and anchor-handling tug types. This is an expansion, not a contradiction.
7. **Ship–tug interaction:** BWK T9 provides detailed bow and stern interaction positions and pilot-boat link. F1 T7 Q33 is a particular berthing scenario; it must not be treated as a full interaction match.
8. **Ocean towing:** BWK T6 includes MSC.Circ.884, bollard-pull test conditions and formulae. No direct F1/F3 answer was found in the inspected pages. Verify current status and applicability of cited guidance before public use.
