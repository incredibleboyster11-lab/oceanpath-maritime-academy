## 2. Detailed overlaps

### R12 ↔ F3 T7 Q48 — MOB rescue turns
- Both sources cover Williamson, Anderson and Scharnow turns.
- BWK R12 additionally covers Lorén turn for heavy-weather recovery and a double turn for a person/marker clearly visible.
- F3 Q48 is a concise answer to the three common manoeuvres and their use cases.
- Do not remove BWK's extra manoeuvres or collapse all turns into one generic answer.

### R11 ↔ F3 T8 Q104 — person fallen overboard in port
- Shared core: raise alarm, throw lifebuoy, inform bridge, use rescue boat if safe, recover the casualty and provide first aid.
- BWK R11 also includes MOB GPS/ECDIS mark, hand steering, whistle signal, lookout, urgency/distress communications, recovery planning and at-sea procedure.
- F3 Q104 includes port control / harbour master and port emergency services, making it a distinct port-specific scenario.

### R10 ↔ F3 T7 Q48 — person missing
- F3 Q48 supplies manoeuvre options, but BWK R10 covers a wider Master’s duty sequence: immediate search, distress alert, company notification, search continuation/termination consultation, inventory of effects and notification to consulate/flag.
- Classify as partial, not duplicate.

## 3. Unmatched areas and learning opportunity

The inspected F3 material does not provide a dedicated equivalent for:
- IAMSAR volumes and SAR coordination levels.
- SMC and OSC responsibilities.
- Datum, search area, track spacing and search-pattern selection.
- Expanding square, sector, parallel sweep, track-line and coordinated ship-aircraft searches.
- MOB in a TSS / restricted visibility.
- Heavy-weather recovery and Lorén turn.
- MRCC/MRSC and Indian SAR organisation.

These are unmatched only in the inspected F3 scope. Do not infer that they are absent from all F3 orals or other source books.
