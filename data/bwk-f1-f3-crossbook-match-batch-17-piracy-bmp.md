## 3. Source-derived observations and review flags

1. **Version conflict:** BWK Z1 explicitly follows BMP5 and notes the Indian Ocean High Risk Area was discontinued from 1 January 2023. F3 T3 Q61 says BMP Maritime Security (2025) replaces BMP5. These statements must not be silently combined; the current edition and applicable regional guidance must be verified.
2. **Reporting vs protection:** F1 provides detailed reporting contacts and UKMTO/MSCHOA procedures. F3 provides more detailed SSP/SSA, SSAS and ship-protection measures. These are complementary layers, not duplicates.
3. **SSAS:** F3 T3 Q59 describes a covert ship-to-shore alert. Preserve the distinction between SSAS activation and audible general/security alarms.
4. **Attack response:** F3 T3 Q58 includes post-attack actions and crew accounting. BWK Z1 has its own approach-stage and attack-stage sequence. Link these but preserve source-specific actions pending qualified security review.
5. **Citadel:** BWK describes a safe muster point/citadel and its limitations; F3 includes internal communication and ability to stop the engine. Do not treat a citadel as a guarantee of military response or a substitute for other ship-protection measures.
6. **AIS:** BWK's version describes AIS remaining on with restricted data; F3 T3 Q61 states AIS on at all times. Preserve the difference and verify current guidance, flag/company policy and applicable security instructions before any operational advice.
7. **Regional threat areas:** BWK's old high-risk-area wording must not be used as a current geographic boundary. The current advisory and reporting area definitions must be checked before publication or voyage use.
8. **Operational security:** Do not publish vessel-specific security plans, citadel details, SSAS activation locations, security weaknesses or sensitive route/vulnerability information.
