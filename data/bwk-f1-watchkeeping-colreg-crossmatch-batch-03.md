## 3. F1 cross-reference corrections

- The watchkeeping-specific F1 matches are mainly in **Topic 9 — Reporting, SOLAS & STCW**, not Topic 1 alone.
- COLREG rule content is mainly in **Topic 1 — ROR / COLREG**.
- Heavy-weather context is partly in **Topic 4 — Meteorology & TRS**.
- Do not flatten these into a single F1 topic or imply every BWK card has one exact F1 duplicate.

## 4. Coverage gaps / cautions

1. BWK W1's four typical bridge-watch levels are not reproduced as a directly equivalent four-level table in the F1 questions inspected.
2. BWK W2's explicit BTM-versus-BRM comparison remains more detailed than the F1 Q1/Q23 cross-reference.
3. BWK W10 has no complete F1 equivalent in the inspected scope; T4 Q86 is equipment-effect coverage, not a complete heavy-weather navigation procedure.
4. BWK W11 contains restricted-water operational details not exhausted by F1 Rule 19 questions.
5. F1 T1 Q30 and T9 Q9 are useful cross-references for W13, but T9 Q9 is a broad SOLAS Chapter V answer, not a dedicated visibility answer.
