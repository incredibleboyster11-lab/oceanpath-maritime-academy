## 2. Useful detailed cross-references

### S4 — flooding and docking lifecycle
- **F3 T6 Q7, printed p. 135:** pre-flooding checks: bottom work and class inspection, plugs, valves, transducers, paint, tank soundings, ballast/stability, openings, rudder/propeller clearance, fire watch and ER watch; stop at first flooding level to check for leaks.
- **F3 T6 Q1, printed p. 133:** critical period and critical instant, upthrust P and virtual loss of GM.
- **F3 T6 Q3, printed pp. 134–135:** Chief Officer's preparation for dry dock.
- **F3 T6 Q63, printed pp. 152–153:** arrival condition, trim/list/GM, plans, tank condition, sea valves, shore connections, risk assessment and class coordination.
- **F3 T6 Q66, printed p. 153:** checks after undocking, including soundings, leaks, critical-period stability, machinery/steering tests, records and PMS.

### S5 — yard emergencies
- **F3 T6 Q46, printed p. 147:** critical-period dangers include loss of stability, excessive aft-block load, hull damage from incorrect block placement, fire/explosion/gas hazards, falls and dropped objects.
- **F3 T6 Q49, printed p. 148:** prevention of toppling: positive GM, correct trim/list, correct blocks, shores, slow pumping, no weight shifts and communication.
- **F3 T6 Q92, indexed in Topic 6 Part B:** response if ship lists while flooding: stop flooding, inform dock master, investigate, check ballast and openings, ensure shores and resume only when safe.
- These are important cross-links but do not replace BWK S5's wider emergency taxonomy and dry-dock safety measures.

### S6 — side-shell plate repair specification
- **F3 T6 Q5, printed p. 134 and Q30, printed p. 142:** shell expansion plan and combined plan information help identify plate/strake, frame location, thickness, steel grade, seams/butts and openings.
- **F3 T6 Q51, printed p. 149:** crack response and class-approved temporary measures; the scenario is a deck crack near hatch cover, not side shell renewal.
- **F3 T6 Q72, printed p. 155:** docking a vessel with bow damage and the class surveyor's renewal scheme; this is damage-specific docking guidance, not a side-shell plate repair specification.
- **F3 T6 Q61, printed p. 152:** general thickness diminution / renewal criteria, subject to class and member location.
- **F3 T6 Q62, printed p. 152:** UTM process, surveyor-selected locations and thickness report.
- The inspected F3 material supports the diagnostic and planning steps, but not the full BWK S6 replacement-plate dimensions and repair specification.

## 3. Source distinctions to preserve

1. BWK S4 is a short flooding checklist. F3 divides the same work into pre-flooding checks, preparation, critical-period stability and post-undocking checks. Keep source question IDs and page provenance rather than merging without a coverage map.
2. BWK S5 is a broad 20-mark dry-dock emergency and safety answer. F3 Q46/Q49/Q92 cover selected stability and flooding hazards, not all yard emergencies.
3. BWK S6 includes specific side-shell insert/renewal dimensions and seam cut-back guidance. The inspected F3 Q51 deck-crack answer is not equivalent and should not be used as a substitute.
4. Numerical repair dimensions and thickness criteria are class-/structure-dependent and require qualified technical review against current class requirements before any public or operational use.
