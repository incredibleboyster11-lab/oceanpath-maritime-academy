## 3. Source-derived observations and review flags

1. **MPX vs passage plan:** P1 is a two-way exchange among Master, pilot and bridge team. F1 passage-planning material supports pilot boarding arrangements, abort points and Master call points but is not a complete MPX answer.
2. **Pilot vs Master:** BWK P2 and P3 distinguish the pilot's conduct of navigation from the Master's command responsibility. Do not reduce the pilot's role to merely giving advice or imply command transfers automatically.
3. **Bridge team monitoring:** F1 BRM material provides general challenge-and-response context. BWK P3 remains the pilotage-specific answer, including position fixes, UKC, helm, rate of turn and RPM monitoring.
4. **Pilot ladder:** F1 T7 Q10 covers heavy-weather ladder precautions; F3 T8 Q17 is an indexed pilot-ladder requirements answer. Verify current SOLAS V/23 and IMO pilot transfer arrangements before public use.
5. **Pilot boarding manoeuvre:** BWK P4 contains operational details for lee-making, current, speed, pilot-boat approach and interaction. No full equivalent was established in F1/F3.
6. **Panama Canal exception:** BWK P2/P3 mention the Panama Canal as an exception to the normal Master-command statement. This is a high-specificity point; verify legal/operational wording and applicability before publication rather than generalising it.
7. **No-match scope:** No direct match established means only that the targeted passages/searches did not surface a full equivalent.
