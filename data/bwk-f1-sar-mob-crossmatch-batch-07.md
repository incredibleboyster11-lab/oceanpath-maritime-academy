## 2. Important differences

1. **IAMSAR structure versus search patterns:** F1 Q16 covers patterns, not IAMSAR's complete purpose, volumes, SC/SMC/OSC levels or OSC duty list.
2. **Search planning:** F1 Q16 names datum-based patterns but does not reproduce BWK R5's complete datum, search-area and track-spacing treatment.
3. **Distress obligations:** F1 T9 Q37 is a strong legal/procedural match to BWK R8. F1 T9 Q34 AMVER is a supporting reporting-system reference only.
4. **MOB turns:** F1 T7 Q27 covers Williamson; BWK R12 includes additional turns. Do not mark the whole turn bank as duplicate.
5. **TSS and fog:** F1 T7 Q19 is TSS-specific; BWK R13 includes TSS and restricted-visibility/fog actions.
6. **Indian SAR organisation:** F1 T3 Q51 provides three MRCC locations and functions; BWK R15 includes further regional authority and MRSC/MRSSC structure.
