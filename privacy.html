Privacy

Respect for every seafarer's data.

Current website status

This first static website release does not provide learner registration, login, newsletter signup, oral-report submission, result submission or candidate-profile sharing. Do not send personal information through this site.

Our intended privacy commitments

  • Collect only information needed for a clearly explained purpose.
  • Ask for separate, affirmative consent for updates and any sharing of a learner profile with a hiring partner. Profile sharing must be off by default.
  • Never sell learner lists or personal data.
  • Do not publish surveyor names or information that could compromise exam integrity.
  • Provide a practical way to withdraw consent and request deletion when data-collection features launch.
  • Keep personal information out of public source-code repositories.
  • Do not open registration to under-18 learners until an appropriate parent/guardian consent process is implemented.

Before forms go live

Any future form will need a purpose-specific notice, data-minimisation review, secure storage, access controls, retention/deletion rules and a tested contact route for privacy requests. This page describes the intended approach; it is not a claim that those systems are already operational or a substitute for legal review.

Independent education resource

OceanPath Maritime Academy is a working brand name for an independent learning initiative. It is not an official DG Shipping, MMD, IMU or government portal, and it does not issue Certificates of Competency.
