How published learning material should be handled

  • Answers should be checked against appropriate official regulations, source documents and calculations.
  • Numerical answers should have reproducible working or check scripts where appropriate.
  • Diagrams should be technically reviewed and checked at desktop and mobile widths.
  • Corrections should identify the affected question/card ID, explain the issue and record the change.
  • Only legitimate past papers and public reports may inform question analysis. Never publish live exam questions or surveyor names.
  • Source-book text must not be copied without permission; learning material should be independently written and properly referenced.

Not an official examination authority

Content is for independent study. It does not guarantee examination results, replace official publications or confer a Certificate of Competency.
