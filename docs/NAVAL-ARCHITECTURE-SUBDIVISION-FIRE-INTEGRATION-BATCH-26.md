## 4. Technical and regulatory review flags

1. The January 2026 SOLAS amendment package includes changes to Chapter II-2, including PFOS-containing firefighting foam prohibition and fuel flashpoint-related provisions. Public fire-integrity / fire-fighting material must be checked against current SOLAS and flag implementation. IMO's amendments page lists the 1 January 2026 effective date. https://www.imo.org/en/about/conventions/pages/amendments-to-imo-instruments.aspx
2. Do not confuse PFOS prohibition with a blanket ban on all fluorinated foams; exact substance scope and applicable instruments must be checked.
3. Passenger subdivision references to SOLAS II-1 need controlled-edition verification and vessel / construction-date applicability.
4. Door class, remote closure, alarm and testing requirements must be checked against ship type and exact regulation before publication.
5. A fire-rated division does not automatically establish watertight integrity, and a watertight division does not automatically establish a particular fire rating.
