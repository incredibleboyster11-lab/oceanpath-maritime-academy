## 2. Key integration insight

The stability content should be arranged as a learning pathway rather than one giant merged answer:

1. **Foundations:** GZ, GM, beam, freeboard, KG, free-surface effect, angle of loll and list.
2. **Curve construction / interpretation:** KN cross-curves, GZ curve, initial GM, dynamical stability and cargo shift.
3. **Regulatory criteria:** general intact stability and severe wind / rolling weather criterion.
4. **Special cargo:** Grain Code criteria, VHM and grain heeling-arm curve.
5. **Dynamic ship behaviour:** rolling period test, unresisted / forced rolling, synchronous rolling and parametric rolling.
6. **Operational application:** stability booklet, loading conditions, free-surface correction and monitoring.

The sequence is an OceanPath instructional architecture inferred from the relationship of the user's source cards; it is not claimed as the original book's exact study order.

## 3. Source-specific differences to preserve

- NA I G01 is a combined Grain Code criteria + VHM answer. NA II D5 is a grain heeling-arm curve answer. Keep separate.
- NA I G11 combines general intact stability and weather criterion; NA II D1 / D2 split these into dedicated cards. A learner should be able to answer either a combined question or the individual sub-question.
- NA I G02 includes beam, freeboard and KG; NA II A3 is titled effect of beam on GZ. Retain the extra scope in NA I.
- NA I G03 explicitly compares angle of loll vs list and remedy; NA II A4 is the angle-of-loll foundation.
- NA I G14 dynamical stability and NA II D1 weather criterion both use GZ-area reasoning, but area definitions and acceptance tests differ. Never substitute one criterion's area for another.
- NA I G15 curve-derived initial GM and NA II C1 rolling-period-test GM are different estimation / interpretation routes.

## 4. Regulatory currency hook

The IMO January 2026 SOLAS supplement lists amendments effective 1 January 2026 affecting SOLAS II-1, II-2 and V. IMO's ship design page identifies SOLAS II-1, the 1966 Load Line Convention and the 2008 IS Code as core ship-design / stability instruments. Any public stability answer that states regulatory thresholds must be checked against the controlled current edition and applicable vessel type. <https://wwwcdn.imo.org/localresources/en/publications/Documents/Supplements/English/QH110E_supplement_January2026.pdf> <https://www.imo.org/en/ourwork/safety/pages/shipdesignandstability-default.aspx>
