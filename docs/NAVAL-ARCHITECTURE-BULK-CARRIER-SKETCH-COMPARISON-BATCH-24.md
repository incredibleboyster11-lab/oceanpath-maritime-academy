## Diagram-level comparison: bulk-carrier midship B15 vs G9

**Date:** 15 November 2026  
**Sources:** NA I B15, printed pp. 96–97; NA II G9, printed pp. 51–52.  
**Status:** Diagram and answer-body comparison completed from source text and extracted diagram descriptions; original PDFs remain read-only.

## 1. Finding — not a safe duplicate merge

The first-pass map treated NA I B15 and NA II G9 as a likely same-vessel sketch candidate. Direct source inspection shows an important difference:

### NA I B15 — single-skin bulk carrier
- Source question: “Sketch and label the mid-ship section of a bulk carrier showing hoppers, topside tanks, double bottom and main structural members.”
- Answer explicitly identifies a **single-skin bulk carrier**.
- Main arrangement: single deck with large hatch openings, hatch coamings / covers, topside wing tanks, hopper side tanks, double bottom, centre girder, side girders, plate floors and hold frames.
- Framing description: bottom, tank top, slopes and deck longitudinally framed; side shell in hold transversely framed.
- Source pages: printed pp. 96–97.

### NA II G9 — bulk carrier / double-skin bulk carrier; high-density cargo strengthening
- Source heading: “Midship section of a bulk carrier / double skin bulk carrier; strengthening for high-density cargo.”
- Diagram description includes steel hatch covers, topside wing tanks, hopper tanks, double bottom with plate and bracket floors, side stringers, stiffeners and transverses, watertight bulkhead.
- Additional high-density cargo strengthening: increased web-frame strength; thicker bulkheads and more stiffeners; strengthened hopper slopes / longitudinals with reduced spacing; increased hopper and inner-bottom scantlings; increased inner-bottom longitudinals and double-bottom floor scantlings; reduced spacing of inner-bottom and bottom longitudinals.
- Source page: printed p. 51 (G9).
- Source asks this in May 2023 Q4 once.

## 2. Corrected relationship

**Classification: related ship-type family / variant, not a confirmed duplicate.**

Do not combine the two source drawings into one canonical sketch:
- NA I B15 explicitly teaches a single-skin bulk carrier arrangement.
- NA II G9 is framed as a bulk carrier / double-skin bulk carrier and adds high-density cargo structural strengthening.
- Some structural members overlap, but the skin arrangement, framing detail, watertight boundaries and strengthening emphasis differ.
- Preserve both answer cards. Create a parent “Bulk carrier midship section variants” concept with two linked child variants, or keep two canonical records linked by a family relationship.
