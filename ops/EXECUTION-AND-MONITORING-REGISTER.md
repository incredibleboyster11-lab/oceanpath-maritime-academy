# OceanPath — Execution & Monitoring Register

**Created:** 15 November 2026  
**Companion:** `docs/MASTER-STRATEGY-RESILIENCE-AND-EXECUTION-BLUEPRINT.md`  
**Purpose:** Turn strategy into observable work. Update this register during each weekly review; conduct a full risk and strategy review quarterly.

## Operating cadence

- **Weekly:** blockers, regulatory alerts, security/backups, top priorities and shipped work.
- **Monthly:** learner outcomes, content quality, audience resilience, institutional discovery, cost and runway.
- **Quarterly:** full risk re-score, scenario review, diversification decisions, recovery drill and roadmap reset.
- **Event-driven:** immediately review material official circulars, incidents, copyright claims, data/security concerns or major platform changes.

## Rules for keeping this register honest

- Use dates and evidence; do not mark work complete because it was discussed.
- Separate planned, in-progress, blocked and verified-complete.
- Every critical/high risk must have an owner, a warning signal and a contingency.
- If a task is blocked, record the blocker and the smallest next action.
- Do not expose learner personal data in this repository.
- Never change the source study repositories under the guise of updating this register.
