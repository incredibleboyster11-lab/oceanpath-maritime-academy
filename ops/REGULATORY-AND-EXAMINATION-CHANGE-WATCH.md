# OceanPath — Regulatory & Examination Change Watch

**Created:** 15 November 2026  
**Owner:** To be assigned  
**Review cadence:** Weekly scan; monthly impact review; immediate triage for material official notices.

## Monitoring scope

1. DGMA / DG Shipping / Nautical Examination notices, circulars, modules, portals, exam delivery and CoC processes.
2. Merchant Shipping Act, rules, regulations and official commencement/effective-date notifications.
3. STCW amendments, IMO circulars, model courses and seafarer training guidance.
4. IMU / MMD / MTI official notices where relevant to learner pathways.
5. Alternative-fuel and decarbonisation training requirements (LNG, methanol, ammonia, hydrogen, LPG, batteries, fuel cells).
6. Official maritime skills, port, shipbuilding and workforce initiatives relevant to future business lines.

## Source hierarchy

- **Tier 1:** Official legislation, gazette notifications, DGMA/DG Shipping circulars/notices, IMO instruments/circulars, official IMU/MMD/MTI notices.
- **Tier 2:** Government ministry/PIB releases and official institutional publications.
- **Tier 3:** Reputable maritime industry reporting used only as an early-warning lead.
- **Tier 4:** Social posts, forums and hearsay — never sufficient evidence for changing exam advice.

## Triage rules

- Verify every material item against a Tier 1 or Tier 2 primary source before changing learner-facing content.
- Record document number, publication date, effective date, scope and exact URL.
- Distinguish proposal, consultation, announcement, issued instrument and effective requirement.
- Within 5 working days of a relevant official change: identify impacted content IDs, assign a reviewer and decide whether to update, label historical, pause or withdraw.
- If safety-critical or exam-critical advice may be wrong, flag or restrict the affected content immediately pending review.
- Keep superseded versions for audit, but never present them as current.
- Do not infer a regulatory requirement from a press release headline alone.
- Each change must have a closure note describing the content and learner-facing actions completed.
